## Found while doing it, and not acted on

Three things turned up that are yours to settle. None has been guessed at.

**1. "PC1 COATS" on the received sheet.** You said in the meeting that coated
material comes back to your own godown. The 21-09 sheet writes `PC1 COATS`
against 168 of the pieces, which reads more like *who coated them* than *where
they are now*. If those pieces are in your godown it is one change; if some are
still sitting at PC1 it is a different one. The question is on the Material
Journey page. Nothing has been assumed either way.

**2. A weight that is shared between two lines.** Your style is to put two or
three colours of one section on the scale together and write the figure against
the first of them. Read row by row that makes the first line heavy and the rest
weightless — which is why 20034 at 16' SG first came out thirty-six per cent
short of what went out. There is no way to recover the true split from the
paper, so for the sent-against-received figures it is divided across the rows it
covers by piece count. Every row that leans on one is marked **shared** and the
page says the percentage beside it is indicative. If you would rather it was
left blank than estimated, say so.

**3. The signal colours were never defined.** Every warning in the system was
styled red and every figure that ties was styled green, and the colour tokens
those styles pointed at did not exist — so the browser dropped them and the
whole dashboard signalled in grey. You have been looking at a system where a
problem did not look like a problem. Fixed; it affects every page.
